## Decisions taken

From the spec:
- **Booked patients in arrivals.** Every booking for today shows in Reception's arrivals from the start. This reverses Figma R06/R07, whose captions were rewritten. R07 now shows the "Matched to CH-1187" path.
- **Health PIN caregiver path.** A family record can be "Managed by" another person on the same number. That person's own Clinica PIN switches to the profile and unlocks its Health, with no second PIN.
- **Reload** (src/app.jsx:684–711, Phase 5b; widened in Phase 9, Run 6 Y05). A real reload restores the tab's whole demo state from sessionStorage: all of `S` (every app's data and where each app was: bookings, payments, check-ins, registered walk-ins, leave decisions, role changes with the audit log, released reports, drafts, each person's record on a shared phone) plus the module-level `staffTab`. Dates (a new hire's start, a leave add-on's end) come back as dates, the visit being written is relinked to its kept draft (and shows "Draft kept"), and Dr. Sharma's walk-ins are added back to her queue's patient list (module data, `drJoin`). Only a real reload restores; a plain navigation starts the demo fresh, and blocked storage is ignored. Real persistence is still out of scope.
